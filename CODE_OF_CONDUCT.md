# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is not a substitute for the full text, which is the binding version.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback on the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **conduct@REPLACE-WITH-PROJECT-DOMAIN** (the owner fills this in
before publication) or privately to any maintainer listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Every report is reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent
ban, depending on the impact and on whether the behavior repeats.
